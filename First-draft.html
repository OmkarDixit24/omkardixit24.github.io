First Draft

Essay • August 2026

It's 2026, been more than a decade since I stopped writing on Quora. After the entry of short form content in form of Music.ly, TikTok, reels and then, shorts around the Pandemic on the social media world, I have this hunch that reading has significantly dropped. I think people were cribbing about dropping reader-ship even long before that, maybe it's been an eternal problem, but ever since we have been hooked on to the short form media - even the ardent of the readers are struggling to maintain the book-reading habbit.

There was a time when Blogger, WordPress were ruling the internet, that has been now taken over by newsletters (including Medium and Substack), but do we really care to read them? I don't know. I don't have the figures.

I used to regularly write on Q, before it turned into a complete mess - a dumpster or rather, a floating patch of garbage in the ocean of internet.

I built this website for uploading the personal projects, to attract the recruiters and hiring managers, but I had this internal itch that was awaiting to be scratched since pandemic. However, writing regularly on Medium/Substack felt daunting, given it is full of "intellectuals", and I wanted a space that was "mine", so, why not this page? couldn't have been better.

But then write on what? I don't know. No idea. I've been out of this game since a long time. I can't just magically write anything, and I consider leisurely writing sacred enough to not let AI or Clanker Quill do this sacred job on my behalf. I'd rather write a few horrible, shitty pages than to let the Clanker write something for me. That's where I draw the line. I'm willing to embrace this uncertainty. Homecoming of a writer. Letting a clanker do this for you is a pure blasphemy.

Anyway, I wanted to pick up the quill and write something down, just to push the boulder uphill, just to get the snowball rolling. Let the momentum build up, and seems like I've managed to come up with a few coherent paragraphs, even if you're wondering what's the purpose of this entire write-up, please, forgive me, for I'm a novice writer, still learning to stitch the fabric of sentences and hoping that this fabric will give some warmth to stranger who ended up in this cold cave of mine.

I hope you also feel the warmth of these words.

I'm leaving you here for now, I'll be back with something new, warmer, thicker.. that will give you the comfort of peeking out of a window on a snowy winter afternoon, with a hot-chocolate fudge in your hands, and a smile on your face. :)
