About Me

A short note on how I think, what I care about, and where I’m headed.

I spend most of my time at the intersection of capital and curiosity—trying to understand how ideas travel, how markets price uncertainty, and how people make decisions under incomplete information.

My background spans investing, writing, and long-form conversation. I host Capital & Curiosity, a podcast and YouTube channel where I sit with founders, investors, and thinkers to unpack ideas that compound. The through-line is simple: better questions tend to produce better outcomes.

Outside of work I read widely (history, biography, and the occasional dense technical paper), write essays that force me to clarify my thinking, and try to stay close to primary sources rather than secondary summaries.

I’m always open to thoughtful conversations. If something here resonates, feel free to reach out.
