About Me

With a foundational background in Computer Science and years spent running content operations end-to-end, I connect business intent with technical reality - mapping how work actually flows, then writing the requirements that close the gap between what leadership wants and what a system can deliver. I'm looking to step into a Business Analyst, Business Systems Analyst, or Product Analyst role where structured thinking meets messy, real-world processes.

Let’s be honest, undocumented processes and unclear requirements quietly stall more projects than bad code ever does. I specialize in fixing both. I thrive on running stakeholder interviews, documenting as-is workflows before proposing to-be ones, and turning ambiguous business asks into BRDs, FRDs, and user stories that engineering and operations teams can actually execute against.

Because of my CS background, I understand what is technically feasible, but my real strength is translating messy business needs into structured, testable requirements - then staying in the loop through UAT to confirm the solution actually solves the problem it was scoped for. If you need someone to run discovery, own the requirements lifecycle, and de-risk decisions with data, we should talk.

Core BA Principles

Requirements Elicitation

Running structured interviews, workshops, and observation sessions to surface what stakeholders need - not just what they ask for.

Process Mapping (As-Is / To-Be)

Documenting current-state workflows, isolating friction points, and designing future-state processes that actually get adopted.

BRD/FRD & User Story Authorship

Turning ambiguous business asks into structured requirements, acceptance criteria, and clear scope boundaries teams can build from.

Gap & Root-Cause Analysis

Using SQL queries and process audits to separate the real bottleneck from the symptom before proposing a fix.

Stakeholder Alignment

Acting as the translation layer between business, engineering, and leadership so a solution actually gets signed off.

UAT & Solution Validation

Owning user acceptance testing and closing the loop between what was scoped and what actually solves the business problem.
