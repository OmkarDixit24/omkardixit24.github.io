Un-Ghosting the Job Market:
Rebuilding Candidate Trust on Indeed

How a 15-day tracking study of 165 applications revealed a 79% ghosting rate—and the automated closure mechanics required to fix it.

In a two-sided marketplace, liquidity dies without trust. When employers are allowed to hoard applications without consequence, the platform turns into a black hole. Here is how we force closure.

Executive Summary

  • The Problem: Indeed’s post-application UX relies on a silent "pull" architecture. My 15-day primary tracking study revealed a 79% ghosting rate, destroying candidate trust.
  • The Solution: "Indeed Loop"—A system featuring Event-Driven Push Notifications, Automated Job Closures for expired listings, and an Algorithmic "Soft Penalty" for habitually unresponsive employers.
  • The Impact: Restores marketplace liquidity by automating updates, forcing definitive psychological closure, and gently disincentivizing application hoarding.

The Trust Crisis

A qualitative analysis of Indeed's Play Store reviews reveals a critical breakdown in marketplace trust. Users complain of "no status change alerts" and "outdated jobs." To validate these qualitative complaints, I conducted a 15-day quantitative tracking study of 165 live applications submitted between October 1st and October 15th.

15-Day Application Tracking Study

Total Applications Submitted 165
Status Updated by Employer 21% (35)
Complete Ghosting (No Movement) 79% (130)

The "Cliffhanger" Effect

The problem isn't just complete ghosting. Even within the 21% that received an update, a significant portion stalled indefinitely at "Application Viewed." The employer opened the resume but never dragged the candidate to a definitive "Rejected" state before the job ultimately expired. The system currently allows employers to leave candidates "on read."

Engineering Closure: The 'Indeed Loop'

To restore marketplace liquidity, the psychological burden of tracking applications must be shifted off the candidate. I evaluated several system-level interventions before prioritizing a three-pillar automation strategy.

Feature Idea Impact Effort Verdict
Strict Employer Mandates Low (Churn Risk) Low Discarded. Alienates the demand side.
Status Sync Push Notifications High Low Selected.

Final Thoughts

Implementing the Automated Closure Protocol and the Soft Penalty means intentionally taking a short-term hit to Indeed's "Total Active Jobs" vanity metric. However, trading a vanity metric for genuine marketplace liquidity and candidate trust is the only way to ensure the platform's long-term survival. No user should ever be left "on read" by a platform.
