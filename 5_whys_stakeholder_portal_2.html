The "5 Whys" Stakeholder Intake Portal

Automated Requirements Elicitation & Backlog Management

An interactive, logic driven web application designed to act as an automated product bouncer. This tool forces stakeholders to articulate their root business problems instead of demanding specific features, effectively eliminating the "XY Problem" before it ever reaches the engineering backlog.

The Core Challenge

In product management, the biggest risk to engineering capacity is building exactly what stakeholders ask for. When operations teams request a complex PDF export button, they often just need a simple dashboard metric. The challenge was to build an intake system that automatically manages scope, extracts the true business value, and translates it into actionable engineering requirements without requiring a manual discovery meeting for every ticket.

System Features & Edge Cases Handled

  • Progressive Root Cause Analysis: Guides users through a structured "5 Whys" framework to unpack surface level requests into core business needs.
  • Integrated RICE Scoring: Forces stakeholders to quantify the proposed feature's Reach, Impact, Confidence, and Effort to ensure data driven prioritization.
  • Automated Agile Synthesis: Dynamically generates perfectly formatted User Stories and Behavior Driven Development (BDD) Acceptance Criteria (Given/When/Then) ready for developer handoff.
  • Friction by Design: Intentionally introduces a "Current Workaround" assessment to filter out low severity requests and protect the development pipeline.
