5 Whys Stakeholder Intake Portal

Interactive Web Application & Elicitation Framework

An interactive, logic driven web application designed to automate requirements elicitation and protect engineering capacity. This tool serves as a structured intake portal that prevents the common "XY Problem", where stakeholders request specific features instead of explaining their underlying business problems.

The Core Challenge

In fast paced environments, stakeholders frequently submit feature requests based on assumptions rather than root causes, leading to bloated backlogs and wasted development cycles. The challenge was to build an automated product bouncer that forces cross functional teams to articulate business value and establish quantifiable metrics before a ticket ever reaches the engineering team.

System Features & Frameworks Applied

  • Root Cause Analysis: Utilizes a progressive disclosure "5 Whys" flow to drill down past surface level feature requests into actual business needs.
  • Quantitative Prioritization: Integrates a real time RICE scoring calculator (Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort) to force stakeholders to quantify the operational impact of their requests.
  • Agile Output Generation: Automatically synthesizes stakeholder inputs into perfectly formatted Agile User Stories.
  • BDD Criteria Builder: Incorporates a Behavior Driven Development (Given / When / Then) intake step, bridging the gap between business requirements and QA testing standards.
