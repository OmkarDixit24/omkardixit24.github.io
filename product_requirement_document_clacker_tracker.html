Product Requirement Document Clacker Tracker

PRD and technical requirements for a hardware product (nRF52840 MCU, BLE companion app); coordinated firmware, hardware, and mobile-app requirements end-to-end

Clacker Tracker is a minimalistic electronic click-logging device designed end-to-end as a 0-to-1 product case study. The hardware captures every physical press with an accurate timestamp, maintains a continuous background clock and daily totals, and stores the data as a structured CSV log on-device. A companion mobile application handles import/export, multi-project assignment, and local organization of that data—keeping the device simple and reliable while pushing flexibility and analysis to software. The project deliberately prioritizes battery life, data integrity, and offline-first use over real-time streaming or cloud accounts. As the product owner, I authored a complete PRD and technical requirements document that coordinated hardware (nRF52840-class MCU, RTC, tactile input, storage), firmware (debounce, CSV append, power management, transfer interface), and mobile software requirements into a single coherent specification. The work also produced interview-ready decision defenses, risk analysis, and prioritization rationale-demonstrating the ability to scope an MVP tightly, make explicit trade-offs, and communicate product and technical decisions clearly to both engineering and hiring stakeholders.
