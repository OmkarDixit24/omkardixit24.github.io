About Me

Connecting the dots between strategy, psychology, and design.

My professional journey began in the world of freelance content strategy and technical writing, where I honed the ability to elicit requirements, document complex workflows, and communicate clearly. However, my curiosity has always extended beyond the written word and into the mechanics of how things are built.

Taking a deliberate career break allowed me to pivot towards my true passion: Product Management and Business Analysis. I approach product design through a unique lens, drawing heavily from my readings in psychology, human influence, game theory, and philosophy. I believe that whether you are designing a digital interface or a physical tool, the core goal is understanding human behavior.

I am deeply influenced by the Feynman Technique and David Perell's philosophies on public writing. I write online to clarify my thinking, process management frameworks, and share my journey in tech.

Beyond software, I have a profound appreciation for minimalist aesthetics—think stark black-and-white layouts, hand-drawn XKCD-style illustrations, and 8-bit retro visuals. In my spare time, I tinker with building minimalistic electronic hardware, studying the mechanics of drones, and exploring embedded systems.
